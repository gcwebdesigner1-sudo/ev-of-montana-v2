FAQ

Questions people ask us.

Is the cleaning really free?

Yes. We clean one room of carpet or hard floor, a couch or a chair at no charge, and you don't have to buy anything.

What's the catch?

While we're there, we show you the Atlantis X for a few minutes and answer questions. That's the whole catch. If it's not for you, say so. You keep the clean room and we head out.

How long does the visit take?

It depends on the room you pick. We'll give you a time estimate when we book you, and the demo part is short.

Do I need to be home?

Yes, and if more than one person decides on big purchases in your house, it helps if they're there too, so nobody has to explain it later.

How much does the Atlantis X cost?

It depends on the package and accessories, so it's covered at your demo or over the phone.

What if I buy one and change my mind?

For in-home sales, you get a written notice when you buy that lets you cancel until midnight of the third business day, as the FTC's Cooling-Off Rule requires.

Is it safe around kids and pets?

It cleans with water only, so there's no chemical residue. The steam is very hot, so keep the nozzle pointed away from people and animals like any hot appliance.

Will it damage my floors or furniture?

It's built for most sealed surfaces, and the low moisture means no soaking. We skip unsealed wood, waxed floors and heat-sensitive materials, and test a hidden spot first.
